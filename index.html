🚨 135K Impressions — 10 Clicks (SEO for HVAC Page)

The SEO for HVAC page ranks at position 31.9 with 135,657 impressions and only 10 clicks. This single page represents 16% of all site impressions but generates almost no traffic. A full content rewrite with case studies, pricing, and FAQ schema could move this to page 1 within 90 days — potentially adding 200–400 monthly clicks.

🚨 Sessions Declining 37% (Jan→Apr)

GA4 sessions fell from 15,908 in January to 10,081 in April — a 37% decline. While part of this is seasonal, the consistent month-over-month drop warrants investigation. Referral traffic (32.7% of sessions) suggests much of this is client portal usage, but organic and direct traffic need to be isolated.

✅ Paid Search — Exceptional 22% Conversion Rate

Paid search generated 669 conversions from 2,990 sessions = 22.4% conversion rate — significantly above the 2–5% industry average for B2B marketing agencies. This signals strong ad-to-landing-page alignment. Scale paid search budget to maximize this high-performing channel.

⚠️ 460K+ Impressions Stranded on Page 3–6

💡 LSA for Roofers — Proof the Strategy Works
