🔧 June Core Update — What To Fix (Content Quality Requirements)
These recommendations are specifically designed to address June 2026 core update signals
❌ Fix 1: Add Named Author Expertise to Every Service Page

June 2026 core update heavily weights "Who wrote this?" signals. Every service page needs: author name, role title (e.g. "LSA Specialist, 6 years"), photo, and a brief bio linking to an author profile page. Example: "Written by [Name], RTM's Google Ads Manager with 8 years managing LSA campaigns for home service companies." This is the fastest EEAT fix available.

❌ Fix 2: Replace Generic Claims With Specific Results

Phrases like "we get results" and "we walk the talk" are flagged as low-quality by Google's quality rater guidelines. Replace with: specific metrics from real clients — "We reduced cost-per-lead from $85 to $34 for a Bradenton HVAC company in 90 days." Add verified Google Review quotes alongside these claims. Vague agency copy = core update penalty.

❌ Fix 3: Add Pricing Transparency

June 2026 core update rewards pages that help users make decisions without contacting. Hidden pricing = low helpfulness score. Add: pricing tiers or ranges for LSA management, GBP management, and SEO. Example: "LSA Management starts at $X/month with no long-term contracts." Google Quality Raters score pages low for withholding information users clearly want.

⚠️ Fix 4: Add FAQPage Schema for AI Overview Eligibility

The June 2026 update also affects AI Overview placements. Service pages without FAQPage schema are ineligible for AI Overview citations. Add 4–6 questions per page: "How much does LSA management cost?", "How long until I see results?", "What industries does RTM serve?", "Is there a contract?" Use JSON-LD format in the page head.

⚠️ Fix 5: Add First-Hand Experience Evidence

Google's E-E-A-T now specifically scores Experience — has the author actually done this work? Add to LSA and GBP pages: screenshots of real campaign dashboards (redacted), photos of team members doing the work, "Behind the scenes" content showing the actual process. Stock photos and generic office images score poorly.

⚠️ Fix 6: Consolidate Thin Duplicate Pages

Multiple LSA pages (/lsa-for-plumbers, /lsa-for-hvac, /lsa-for-roofers etc.) may be creating topic dilution signals if they share 70%+ identical content. Each page must have truly unique content: industry-specific cost-per-lead benchmarks, industry-specific verification requirements, and industry-specific case studies. Template-swapped content is a core update trigger.
